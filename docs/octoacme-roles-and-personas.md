# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy, test planning, and validation across the project. They ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and are production-ready before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and test plans aligned with project scope
- Create and maintain test cases and automation frameworks
- Lead QA activities (manual and automated testing)
- Identify defects, document bugs, and verify fixes
- Collaborate with Developers on testability and coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria before marking work as done
- Report quality metrics and risk signals to the PM and Product Lead

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, defect-free releases
- Reduce post-release incidents and customer impact
- Improve test coverage and reduce manual testing effort

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics in weekly syncs
- Test strategy discussions during planning
- Defect reporting and triage in issue tracking
- Release readiness sign-off before deployment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to ensure that features are testable and regressions are caught early
- Partners with the Product Manager on acceptance criteria and release quality gates
- Supports Project Managers with risk visibility and readiness checkpoints for milestones

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, ensure technical quality, and mentor the engineering team on design and best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Define coding standards, patterns, and best practices
- Mentor Developers on technical excellence and design
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Validate solutions against scalability and performance requirements
- Collaborate with other technical leads on cross-team integration points

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable architecture
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team growth through mentorship

### Typical Communication
- Design review comments and architecture docs
- Technical guidance in code reviews
- Architecture discussions during planning
- Cross-team technical sync meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides technical direction to Developers and helps translate business priorities into feasible design decisions
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and technical dependencies
- Works with QA/Testing Lead to ensure architecture supports observability, resilience, and testability

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate team processes, remove blockers, and coach the team on agile practices. They enable the team to be self-organizing and continuously improving.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retro)
- Remove or escalate blockers that impede team progress
- Coach the team on agile practices and process adherence
- Maintain sprint board and backlog health
- Identify process improvements and track action items
- Support retrospective facilitation and continuous improvement culture

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Remove impediments to delivery

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Process improvement proposals
- Blocker escalation when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers by improving flow and reducing process friction
- Works with Project Managers to keep delivery rhythms aligned with the plan
- Helps Product Managers and stakeholders understand team capacity, trade-offs, and dependency impacts

---

## Executive Sponsor/Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide business context, high-level prioritization, and governance oversight. They champion the project at the leadership level and ensure alignment with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Provide executive sponsorship and remove organizational barriers
- Approve major changes in scope, timeline, or budget
- Attend milestone reviews and provide strategic feedback
- Escalate and resolve cross-functional dependencies
- Communicate project status to leadership and stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers business value
- Maintain strategic alignment and governance
- Enable team autonomy within business constraints

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone reviews and approval gates
- Escalation and decision-making forums
- Strategic alignment discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns Product Managers and Project Managers on business priorities and executive signals
- Reviews progress with PMs and supports cross-functional decisions that affect scope or milestones
- Provides visibility to senior leadership without directly managing day-to-day delivery work

---

## Product Operations

### Role Summary
Product Operations manages project tools, dashboards, metrics, and operational workflows. They enable data-driven decision-making and process efficiency.

### Responsibilities
- Configure and maintain project management tools (for example, GitHub Projects or Jira)
- Build and maintain dashboards for project metrics and health signals
- Track and report velocity, burndown, and key success metrics
- Support process automation and tool integration
- Ensure project data accuracy and hygiene
- Provide insights and recommendations based on project data

### Goals
- Enable visibility and data-driven decisions
- Reduce manual overhead and process friction
- Provide actionable insights to inform prioritization and planning

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reviews with PM and Product Manager
- Tool configuration and support requests
- Data-driven insights and trend analysis
- Operational efficiency recommendations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers by making metrics and reporting easier to trust and act on
- Helps Project Managers maintain clean status reporting and cross-team visibility
- Coordinates with Developers and QA when instrumenting health checks, dashboards, and release metrics

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles create a complete delivery model: Product and business leadership define outcomes, PMs and Scrum Masters enable flow, Technical Leads and Developers build the solution, QA ensures quality, and Product Operations provides visibility and operational support.
